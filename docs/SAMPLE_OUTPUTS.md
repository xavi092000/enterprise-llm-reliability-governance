\# Sample Evaluation Findings



\# Example 1 — Stable Prompt



\## Prompt



PEPD1 — Revenue Growth Acceleration



\## Observation



The prompt remained structurally stable across runs with:



\- strong KPI consistency,

\- low semantic drift,

\- and stable executive recommendations.



\## Reliability Finding



The combination of:



\- deterministic formatting,

\- KPI constraints,

\- and grounding rules



significantly improved reliability.



\---



\# Example 2 — Grounding Weakness



\## Observation



Some prompts produced unsupported conclusions when source documents lacked sufficient evidence.



\## Reliability Finding



The issue was not always caused by prompt quality.



In several cases, the source document itself lacked the required KPI coverage.



This led to:



\- source coverage validation,

\- evidence confidence handling,

\- and stricter grounding constraints.



\---



\# Example 3 — Semantic Drift



\## Observation



Repeated executions occasionally produced:



\- different prioritizations,

\- different recommendations,

\- and inconsistent strategic focus.



\## Reliability Finding



Enterprise GenAI systems require drift monitoring and evaluation workflows to maintain output consistency.



\---



\# Key Insight



The project demonstrates that enterprise GenAI reliability depends on:



\- grounding,

\- evaluation,

\- monitoring,

\- governance,

\- and iterative prompt hardening.
